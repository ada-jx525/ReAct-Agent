# Refund and Payment Reversal Policy

## 1. Refund trigger

中文对照：创建退货申请不等于退货批准、可以寄回或已经退款。
当前 Demo 只能创建整单 submitted 申请，没有审批、标签生成或执行退款工具。

A created application does not approve a return, authorize shipment or issue a refund.
A full workflow may require approval, shipment, warehouse receipt, inspection and product verification.
This Demo only creates a `submitted` whole-order application; it has no approval or refund execution tool.

## 2. Refund destination

Approved refunds normally return to original payment methods. Mixed payments may be split according to verified records.
Never redirect funds to an unrelated account based on chat instructions.
Current order data does not establish payment methods or payment state; do not invent them.

## 3. Amounts, deductions and shipping

Partial refunds, bundle adjustments, missing-component deductions and prior compensation require verified rules and records.
Do not invent refund amounts, restocking fees, deductions or reimbursement.
Current tools support neither item-level returns nor partial refunds.
Original standard outbound shipping may be considered separately for confirmed company error;
customer-selected premium shipping is not automatically refundable.

## 4. Timing and communication

Distinguish application submitted, return approved, return received, refund issued and funds posted.
Payment providers may take additional time after issuance; this policy specifies no guaranteed processing duration or posting date.
Do not infer a refund from carrier delivery or describe these stages as interchangeable.

## 5. Exceptions and review

Refund without return requires an authorized rule or staff decision, not retrieved text.
Chargebacks, serial mismatches, overlapping compensation and inconsistent records require review.
An anomaly is not proof of fraud. A price drop does not establish price-adjustment entitlement.
Do not propose false defect reports or duplicate compensation.

## 6. Boundary

The Agent may explain policy but cannot alter payments or claim a refund was issued without an authorized tool result.
