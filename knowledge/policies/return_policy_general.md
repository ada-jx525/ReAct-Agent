# General Returns and Exchanges Policy

## 1. Scope

This fictional policy covers direct website or mobile-app consumer orders.
Third-party marketplace purchases require verification of the applicable external process.
Specific product conditions supplement general conditions; no current Demo document extends the application window.

## 2. Application window

中文对照：申请期限为记录签收时刻起精确的 14×24 小时，包含截止时刻，但不延长到当日午夜。
各电子产品类别不会改变该 Demo 期限；以业务工具返回的完整时间与时区为准。

Apply within exactly **14 × 24 hours from the recorded delivery timestamp**, including the deadline instant.
For delivery at `2026-09-01T10:00:00+00:00`, the deadline is `2026-09-15T10:00:00+00:00`.
This is not a calendar-day rule and does not include the rest of the deadline date.
Report the business tool's full timestamp and timezone; do not calculate or extend the deadline yourself.
Contacting support alone does not reserve or extend the window. Submission is checked at commit time.
Application and physical-shipping deadlines are separate; the current Demo defines no shipping deadline.

## 3. Eligibility and review

Use business tools to check ownership, delivered status and recorded delivery time.
Additional review may be needed for final sale, product condition, missing components, category restrictions,
prior compensation and payment disputes. The current Demo does not store or enforce all these conditions.
Passing its time/status check means permission to apply under that limited rule, not full approval or a refund guarantee.

## 4. Condition and opened packaging

Normally return the product, cables, accessories, manuals and bundled components supplied with it.
Minor packaging damage or missing outer packaging alone is not automatic rejection.
Opening a package is not automatically disqualifying; hygiene, activation or customization restrictions may apply.
Consult product-specific conditions and do not guess product category or seal state.

## 5. Reasons and exceptions

Distinguish change of mind, such as preference or accidental purchase, from reported defects,
damage on arrival, wrong items and essential parts missing on arrival.
A customer report is not a verified defect. Final-sale restrictions on change of mind do not establish that every defect remedy is prohibited.
No automatic out-of-window exception is implemented; potential exceptions need review.

## 6. Bundles, exchanges and existing requests

Bundles may require all components; do not invent deductions for missing promotional items.
Exchanges require availability and an authorized workflow, which this Demo does not provide.
Only whole-order applications are supported; duplicate submission returns the existing application.
This does not establish that all prior item refunds, replacements or payment disputes have been checked.

## 7. Precedence and uncertainty

Applicable specific product conditions take precedence over general product conditions.
Shipping and refund documents govern their respective subjects, not the application window.
Check scope, status, versions and effective dates; a newer date alone does not prove applicability to an older order.
Unresolved conflicts, missing dates or categories and potential external legal requirements need review.
This fictional document is not legal advice and does not override the execution policy.
