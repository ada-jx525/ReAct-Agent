# Electronics and Audio Returns Policy

## 1. Scope and window

Covers direct-sale headphones, earbuds, speakers, watches, tablets, keyboards, mice, chargers and cables.
Large appliances, enterprise hardware and marketplace purchases are outside this Demo scope.
All covered categories use the general **14 × 24 hour** application window; none extends it.
The conditions below require evidence beyond the current deterministic time/status check.

## 2. Opened over-ear and on-ear headphones

Opened over-ear and on-ear headphones may normally be considered within the window.
Include supplied cables, cases, adapters and essential components. Missing minor packaging alone is not rejection.
Do not guarantee acceptance before product-condition review.

## 3. In-ear headphones and earbuds

中文对照：未拆封、保持出厂密封的入耳式耳机，可在一般申请窗口内考虑退货。
已经拆封且卫生封条破损的入耳式耳机，通常不接受无理由退货；未使用也不自动构成例外。
若报告功能缺陷、支持条件下的充电或配对失败、错发商品、缺少配件或到货损坏，可进一步审核。
质量问题例外不等于已获批准，也不会自动延长申请期限。

Factory-sealed in-ear products may normally be considered within the general window.
Opened in-ear products with a broken hygiene seal are generally not accepted for change-of-mind returns.
However, reported functional defects, charging/pairing failures under supported conditions,
wrong items, missing components or damage at delivery may warrant review after opening.
Opening alone must not reject a reported defect; these exceptions do not automatically extend the window.
If product type or seal condition is unknown, request only the missing information.

## 4. Defects versus preference

Potential defects include failure to power on or charge, a failed audio channel, faulty buttons and damage at delivery.
Sound preference, discomfort or color preference are normally change-of-mind reasons.
Unsupported configurations do not by themselves establish a defect.
Preserve the customer's explanation; do not reclassify it to obtain eligibility or free shipping.

## 5. Troubleshooting and accidental damage

Reasonable troubleshooting may include charging, restarting, resetting and testing supported cables or devices.
Do not delay an application beyond its window or require unrelated troubleshooting for visible delivery damage.
Post-delivery liquid/impact damage, lost parts and misuse are not normally covered by standard returns.
Repair, warranty and protection-plan options require separate verification; current tools do not implement them.

## 6. Components, serials and privacy

Missing essential charging cases, proprietary adapters or modules requires review, not a guaranteed full refund.
Serial mismatch requires review, not an automatic fraud accusation or refund.
Customers may need to back up data, remove account locks and reset supported devices before physical return.
Never request account passwords or device-unlock credentials in chat.

## 7. Operational boundary

ORD-1001's `Wireless Headphones` name does not prove in-ear, on-ear or over-ear design.
The current system cannot verify category, seal state, serials or defects.
Explain conditions without claiming missing facts are verified or full eligibility is established.
