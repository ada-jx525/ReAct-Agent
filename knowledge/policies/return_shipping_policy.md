# Return Shipping and Carrier Policy

## 1. Shipping responsibility

中文对照：经确认的商品质量或履约问题，通常由公司承担合理标准退货运费；无理由退货通常由客户承担。
客户报告质量问题不等于已确认；原因不明确时，运费责任仍待核实，不可承诺免费退货。

The company normally covers reasonable standard return shipping for a confirmed product or fulfillment issue:
damage on arrival, material defect, wrong item, missing essential parts at delivery or duplicate fulfillment.
Customers normally pay for change of mind unless a verified promotion or membership exception applies.
A reported defect is not a confirmed defect. Unclear classification leaves responsibility unresolved.
Do not change reason classifications solely to shift costs or obtain free shipping.

## 2. Labels and unsupported operations

In a full service, an authorized logistics workflow may issue a label after the applicable approval steps.
Labels must bind the customer, order, items and return request.
**This Demo has no label-generation or pickup tool. Submission does not generate a label.**
Do not claim a label, address, booking or reimbursement has been issued, or advise shipping without verified instructions.

## 3. Label validity

An issued label has its own printed validity terms. The fictional default is 14 calendar days after issuance.
This is separate from the order's 14 × 24 hour application window.
The Demo neither issues labels nor calculates label expiry. An expired label does not automatically invalidate an approved return.
Replacement labels require an authorized logistics process.

## 4. Carrier, tracking and packaging

Use the approved label's carrier and service; unapproved or premium shipping may not be reimbursed.
Retain tracking and proof of shipment. Warehouse delivery does not establish inspection or refund completion.
Use padding, secure closure and protection for loose components; original packaging is preferred but not always mandatory.
Current tracking tools show outbound Demo snapshots, not newly created return shipments.

## 5. Exceptions and reimbursement

Lost authorized shipments need evidence and investigation. International and restricted-goods shipping require specialist instructions.
Do not invent customs reimbursement or advise ignoring carrier restrictions.
Incorrectly charged shipping may be reviewed with receipts, tracking and itemized costs.
Current tools cannot calculate, approve or pay shipping reimbursement.

## 6. Boundary

Shipping guidance does not establish product eligibility or authorize a business write.
